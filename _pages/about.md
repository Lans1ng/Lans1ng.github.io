I am a Ph.D. candidate in Information and Communication Engineering at [Southwest Jiaotong University](https://www.swjtu.edu.cn/), advised by Prof. [Turgay Celik](https://www.wits.ac.za/staff/academic-a-z-listing/c/turgaycelikwaborloaborloaborlasaborjupanelwaborloaborloaborlasaborjupanelwitsaborloaborloaboracaborjupaneljupaneljupanel/) and Prof. [Hengchao Li](https://faculty.swjtu.edu.cn/lihengchao/zh_CN/index.htm). My research focuses on **remote sensing image analysis** and **computer vision**, particularly in:

- Few-shot Object Detection
- Semi-supervised Object Detection
- Domain Adaptation
- Weakly-supervised Segmentation

From February 2023 to December 2024, I was a research intern at [A*STAR](https://www.a-star.edu.sg/) in Singapore, working at Infocomm Research (I2R) and Centre for Frontier AI Research (CFAR).
